Fable Lab: four showcase concepts

One brand, one goal (book the call), four color worlds: amber-on-carbon, cyan-on-newsprint, teal-on-deep-space, ink-on-porcelain. All procedural, every number real and sourced.

COMMAND

The war room

Carbon black + amber phosphor. A constellation of 160 real candidates collapses through the funnel into one pulsing hire as the 41-day search ticks down.

 MANIFESTO

The broadside

Newsprint, black ink, a cyan marker, and a red stamp. The strike line draws itself through "resumes"; plates snap into register.

 ATLAS

The talent map

A 3D point-cloud Earth. Pick your path and watch arcs stream global talent across the planet into your team.

 ONE

The art piece

A generative ink drawing that draws itself as you scroll: noise, current, three, one. Porcelain, ink, and a single vermilion seal.
