The correction

The method, typeset in five steps.

01

Briefing

A 45 to 60 minute alignment call. The seat, the territory, the comp, what a great first year looks like.

02

Market mapping

The full landscape for the role, including the passive candidates who are not answering job ads.

03

Multi channel outreach

We go to the market directly, on every channel it actually reads.

04

SELLER assessment

A scored two step interview. Every candidate measured against the same six dimensions.

05

Shortlist to signed offer

3 to 5 finalists, interviews coordinated through to a signed offer. Weekly update meetings with a live client portal throughout.

  • Typical search runs 4 to 6 weeks.
  • Replacement guarantee up to two years. Under 5% of placements ever need it.
  • Commercial insurance, manufacturing, B2B tech and SaaS.

Path two / global

Global Talent Solutions

  • Offshore SDRs, BDRs, AEs, account managers, customer success, RevOps.
  • 60 to 80% cost savings vs comparable US hires.
  • Typically placed in under 4 weeks.
  • Direct hire or subscription, with 6 month replacement coverage.
